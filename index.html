The Philosophy

How Do I Fix People and Process Leaks?

Using my proven 12-step approach:

  1. Face Reality
    By accepting things as the way they are, with brutal honesty, anything becomes possible
  2. The Do, Own, and Risk Trap
    By trusting your team and playing one role in your business (no more many hats) everything becomes easier
  3. Operating Values vs Core Values
    You need a decision-making framework more than a values framework
  4. Run a Business You Could Sell
    Your asset should be more valuable over time
  5. The Talent Siphon
    You need a labor advantage over your competitors, never overpaying your employees
  6. Command by Intent
    Your people need to know how to make decisions without you in the room
  7. The Daily Bookend
    You need to run a daily business, the goal being winning one day at a time
  8. Mistakes Mean Progress
    You're building a learning company and you need a team that remains calm in a storm
  9. Systems Thinking
    Your people need to be better than AI at the human parts
  10. Labor Efficiency Ratio (LER)
    A simple measure to define the relationship between dollars out and dollars in
  11. Junior Talent Is Your Future
    You need seniors who can teach your juniors
  12. What Winning Looks Like
    Progress is more important than perfection — you don't need to win by knockout

The Offering

Fit Comes First

I work with clients I believe in and who share my vision for running a successful, profitable agency. Which is not everyone. If the following applies to your business, we might be a good fit:

  1. You're Tired of Living This Way

    Although you're a decade into your business, things feel tougher and less predictable than ever, especially with technology racing away from you at the moment you can least afford it. You want to change for better once and for all

  2. You're Tired of Making Less Than You're Worth

    You're not getting the margins nor compensation you deserve for your efforts. You're too important in the company and not important enough in the marketplace

  3. You Want The Option to Sell

    Things seem to work out but there's no written process. Your team is smart but you're worried that key players leaving would mean losing your entire knowledge base. You know that needs to change, and that means you need to change

  4. You Take Your Work (And Not Yourself) Seriously

    Your professional standards are high and you care about your people, your clients, and your output. But you have a sense of humor about yourself and understand you're part of an ecosystem, that making a big difference to a small group is what matters most
